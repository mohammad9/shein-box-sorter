Add a customer's cart

  1. Copy the customer's Shein cart link.
  2. Open Safari, paste the link into the address bar and go. If Safari asks to open the SHEIN app, tap Cancel.
  3. If Shein shows a "confirm you're human" box, tick it and wait until you see the products.
  4. Open your bookmarks and tap Get SKUs. This app opens with the cart's items.
  5. Type the customer's name and tap Add.

On a PC: add carts, then send them to the scanner

  1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it: Get SKUs
  2. Open a customer's Shein cart link. When the products show, click Get SKUs in the bookmarks bar and type the customer's name. Repeat for each customer.
  3. Go to Customers, click Send to scanner, and send the short link to the person who scans the box. Their scans then show up live on your PC.

One-time on iPhone: install the "Get SKUs" button

  1. Tap Copy button code below.
  2. In Safari tap the Share icon, then Add Bookmark. Name it Get SKUs and save.
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap Get SKUs, clear the address and paste the copied code. Tap Done.

Tips

  • Add this app to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to open it like an app.
  • Everything is saved on this phone only.
  • If a product has the same SKU for two customers in different sizes, the app asks you which size the label shows.
